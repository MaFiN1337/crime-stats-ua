## 1.Legal Basis and Essence of the Crime

Fraud in Ukraine (or **Froad**) is classified under **Article 190 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine** as the **taking of another's property or the acquisition of the right to property by deception or breach of trust.**

The key characteristic of this crime is the **intentional misleading of a victim** so that they **voluntarily transfer** their property, the right to it, or financial funds.

## 2.Analysis of Fraud Dynamics and Structure

Analysis of the provided data from the **Fraud Payments Tracker** and **Incident Database (БД Інциденти)** reveals clear trends in financial fraud.

### 2.1. Structure of Fraudulent Operations

The structure of the total sum of fraudulent operations confirms that methods using psychological influence cause the greatest financial damage:

| Fraud Type | Amount (UAH) | Share | Conclusion |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Social Engineering** | 318,267,009 | **57%** | Dominant method resulting in the largest financial losses. |
| **Unauthorized Payment (НСП)** | 182,891,213 | **33%** | Significant share related to data theft and illegal write-offs. |
| **Non-delivery of Goods/Services** | 55,171,887 | **10%** | Least significant share. |

### 2.2. Dynamics of Fraudulent Operations by Type
![Опис зображення](info/190/fraud_operations_dynamic.png)

* **Social Engineering** (orange) is the main factor driving fluctuations in the total sum of fraudulent operations. Peaks occurred in **July 2023** (over 20 million UAH) and **November 2024** (around 20 million UAH).
* The sum of **Unauthorized Payments (НСП)** remains relatively stable, fluctuating around 5 million UAH, consistently representing a significant 'baseline' loss.

### 2.3. Incident Registration
![Опис зображення](info/190/incidents.png)

* There is a clear trend of **growth in the number of organizations** registering incidents (black line, right axis), which rose from ~4 to **~20 organizations** by the end of 2024. This indicates expanding coverage of the Incident Database service and increased cooperation.
* The number of incidents (red line) shows significant peaks, with the highest recorded in **November 2024** (~4,300 incidents), correlating with the peak sum of fraud caused by social engineering (Fig. 3).

### 2.4. Effectiveness of the Cardholder Verification Service
![Опис зображення](info/190/cardholder_usage.png)

The Cardholder Verification service (checking cardholder-document compliance) demonstrates increasing effectiveness:

* The **number of discrepancies** (columns, left axis) shows a clear upward trend from early 2024, peaking at **~700 cases** in January 2025.
* The **estimated amount of prevented fraud losses** (line, right axis) rises parallel to the number of discrepancies, reaching **4 million UAH** in January 2025. This shows that verification mechanisms are working and **successfully preventing fraud** that relies on dummy or non-compliant data.

## 3.Factors and Hypotheses on Fraud Mechanisms

Factors (mechanisms) influencing the commission of fraud:

| Factor | Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Development of Digital Fintech** and growth of cashless payments | Creation of new remote deception tools. |
| **Digitalization of Society** and **Low Cyber Literacy** | High effectiveness of social engineering. |
| **Digitalization of Government Services** | Exploitation of identification vulnerabilities. |

### Summary of Hypotheses

1.  **Hypothesis 1 (Fintech Development):** The growing popularity of online shopping increases the "attack surface" for fraudsters (phishing, fake stores), which is reflected in the significant share of **Unauthorized Payments (33%)**.
2.  **Hypothesis 2 (Low Cyber Literacy):** **Social Engineering (57%)** dominates due to low public awareness, allowing fraudsters to easily persuade victims (vishing) to disclose confidential data under the guise of "protection."
3.  **Hypothesis 3 (Digitalization of Government Services):** The increase in discrepancies found by the **Cardholder Verification** service suggests that fraudsters are actively exploiting identification vulnerabilities (e.g., controlling a victim's phone number) to access data and apply for microloans in the victim's name.

---

## 4.General Conclusion

The dynamics of fraud in Ukraine clearly correlate with the pace of **digitalization** and the **low level of public cyber literacy**. The greatest financial damage is caused by **social engineering methods (57%)**, underscoring the need to strengthen not only technical protection but also **educational campaigns**. The growing effectiveness of the **Cardholder Verification** service proves that existing interbank tools are valuable in combating fraud that uses fraudulent data.
